# AliBeka acceptance tests

## Test 1 - Quick-record a bale and create draft pieces [sanity]
Viewport: mobile
Covers: quick bale intake, category counts, draft creation and persistence.
Steps:
1. Open Receive & prepare → Receive bale.
2. Enter purchase date, purchase price and supplier.
3. Enter counts across categories and create category drafts.
4. Open Stock and filter to Needs details.
Expected: the bale is recorded and one draft exists for every counted piece, grouped by category.

## Test 2 - Complete a draft piece and attach a photo
Viewport: mobile
Covers: draft completion, availability transition and photo upload.
Steps:
1. Open a draft piece.
2. Enter name/category/size/quality.
3. Add a real image.
4. Verify the editor remains open after the photo is saved.
5. Save the piece.
Expected: the editor does not close when the photo is added; the piece becomes available and the photo is visible in Stock.

## Test 3 - Replace or remove an item photo
Viewport: desktop
Covers: photo replacement, deletion and immediate editor state.
Steps:
1. Open an available item with a photo.
2. Replace the photo.
3. Verify the same editor remains open and the new image is visible.
4. Remove the photo.
5. Verify the editor remains open and the image disappears immediately.
6. Save/close and reopen the item.
Expected: replacement and removal happen without navigating away; removal persists while category, size, quality and price remain unchanged.

## Test 4 - Adjust a negotiated POS price quickly
Viewport: mobile
Covers: POS cart and negotiated sale price.
Steps:
1. Open Sell and add an item priced at KSh 1,000.
2. Apply -100 then +50.
Expected: actual sale price is KSh 950 while base price remains KSh 1,000.

## Test 5 - Complete a sale and remove the item from available inventory
Viewport: mobile
Covers: checkout, payment and sold transition.
Steps:
1. Add an available item to the cart.
2. Choose payment and select Cash.
3. Return to Stock.
Expected: the sale is recorded and the item is no longer available.
